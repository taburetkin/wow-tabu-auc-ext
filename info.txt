Summary:
On the sell panel, shows the auction house cut (5% at your faction, 15% at a neutral auctioneer) and the gold you keep. Green when that beats the vendor, red when the vendor pays as much or more.

Description:
While you type a price, the sell panel shows what the auction house takes and what you keep.

- House cut is 5% at a faction auctioneer and 15% at a neutral one.
- You receive is the stack price minus that cut.
- Next to it is the difference versus selling the same items to a vendor. Green when the auction pays more, red when the vendor pays the same or more. From 1 gold up, only the gold shows; hover for the exact amount.
- The auction total stays where it is. These two rows sit under it.

The unit price is filled in for you. The color of the digits says where that number came from, and hovering them shows the same note.

- **Purple:** the lowest buyout of this affix.
- **Blue:** this affix is not listed, so this is the cheapest listing of the same item.
- **White:** the item is not gear and it is already listed. This is the auction house minimum.
- **Red:** the item is not listed. The auction house suggests its own minimum: the vendor price times 1.5, rounded up to the next silver.

There is no scan. One option, off until you turn it on: a stack of more than one can show the vendor price of a single item on the item tooltip, drawn like Sell Price. WoW Forever only.
